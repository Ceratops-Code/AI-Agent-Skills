## Contract Review Rules

- Read the owning action's source-doc registry and in-scope contracts before
  reviewing standards; identify the requirement whose currency, placement, or
  scope needs review.
- Keep durable standards in the owning skill's contracts and source-doc
  registry. Keep machine-checkable requirements in deterministic contracts and
  judgment-dependent requirements in review contracts.
- Use registered official sources as standards authority and reference
  implementations only as bounded pattern evidence for the standards question.
- Review contract definitions without treating them or the review as evidence
  that a repository or skill complies with those standards.
- After approved edits, verify contracts, checker references, the source-doc
  registry, and contract-structure docs remain aligned, with consistent check
  IDs, evidence mappings, and remediation classifications.
